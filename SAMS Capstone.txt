## ⚠️ One Honest Caveat

manuscript format vs. a full thesis format, some require a specific number of ISO 25010
evaluators, some use their own custom rubric instead of pure ISO/IEC 25010, and
formatting/binding/submission rules vary by institution. **Confirm the exact requirements
with your adviser directly** — this checklist covers what's implied by your own paper and
system, not your program's exact administrative rules.
